About Scout

Autonomous Research Intelligence

Scout is an autonomous research and report generation agent engineered to retrieve, refine, and synthesize information from the open web. It orchestrates a multi-stage pipeline — semantic web extraction, relevance filtering, and structured report composition — to deliver accurate, citation-backed intelligence on demand.

Powered by specialized large language models tuned for research workflows, Scout operates with minimal supervision, transforming a single query into a comprehensive, well-sourced analytical brief within seconds.
