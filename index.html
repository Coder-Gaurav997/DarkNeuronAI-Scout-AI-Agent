About Scout

Autonomous Research Intelligence

Scout is an autonomous research agent that retrieves, refines, and synthesizes information from across the open web. It runs a multi-stage pipeline — semantic extraction, relevance filtering, and structured report composition — to deliver accurate, citation-backed briefs on demand.

Powered by specialized language models tuned for research workflows, Scout works with minimal supervision, turning a single query into a comprehensive brief in seconds.
